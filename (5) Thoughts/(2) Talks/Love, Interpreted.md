<p align="justify">For some people, love is found in romance, in late-night conversations, gentle touches, shared laughter, and the comfort of being close to someone. For others, love is something so much quieter. It's found in loyalty, patience, and understanding one another. Perhaps that's why love has never been that simple. It's far more complicated than you actually think, it can be understood in countless ways. The truth is, love is subjective. You can't simply say that love is one thing or another, because every person has their own interpretation of what it means. Each person experiences it differently, giving it a unique meaning shaped by their own life and experiences.</p>
<p align="justify">To me, one of the most beautiful forms of love is having someone who remains by your side through every ups and downs of your life. Someone who celebrates your victories without any envy in sight and stays, supports you even when you're down on your knees without judgement. Their presence becomes a source of comfort, making the fears and uncertainties you've carried all the time seem a little less overwhelming.</p>
<p align="justify">Love can also be the reason that quietly changes the way you see yourself. Sometimes, meeting the right person doesn't make your life strive overnight, but it inspires you to grow. To give you a lesson that the beauty of life isn't found within its accomplishments, its journey makes the beauty of it. Their kindness teaches you to be kinder. Their faith in you encourages you to trust yourself again. Without ever realising it, you begin chasing goals you once had abandoned and believing in possibilities once again.</p>
<p align="justify">At the end of the day, love can't be defined by a single concept. It exists in infinite forms, shaped by different experiences and perspectives. It's you who decides what kind of definition of love you want to accept, fight for, and hold onto.</p>
