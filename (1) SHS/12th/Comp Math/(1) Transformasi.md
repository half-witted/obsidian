## Pengertian
Perubahan posisi, bentuk, atau ukuran suatu objek
## Jenis
Urut nomer
### Vertikal
| <center>No</center> | <center>Jenis</center>     | <center>Fungsi</center>                                              | <center>Keterangan</center>                                                    |
| ------------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| <center>1</center>  | Rotasi (counter-clockwise) | $90^\circ = (-y,x)$<br>$180^\circ = (-x,-y)$<br>$270^\circ = (y,-x)$ | $-90^\circ = 270^\circ$<br>$-180^\circ = 180^\circ$<br>$-270^\circ = 90^\circ$ |
| <center>2</center>  | Dilatasi                   | $y = kf(x), (x,ky)$                                                  | Skala k $Dy(k)$                                                                |
| <center>3</center>  | Refleksi                   | $y = -f(x)$                                                          | Sumbu x                                                                        |
| <center>4</center>  | Translasi                  | $y = f(x)\pm b$                                                      | $+b =$ atas<br>$-b =$ bawah                                                    |
### Horizontal
