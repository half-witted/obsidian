Promised myself I'd walk this road alone,
to focus on me, to grow, to claim my own.
Trying to resist the pull, the fleeting mark,
keeping my heart safe, shielding from the dark.

But then you smiled, so warm, so rare,
a quiet storm, the softest air.
Your eyes, holding stories unspoken,
pulled me in, a spell unbroken.

I tried to fight in, stand my ground,
but in your gaze, my strength unwound.
Falling swifter, my plans left behind,
reaching for dreams that weren't mine to find.

I did my best to make you mine,
but sometimes love just doesn't align.
In that silent truth, sharp yet sweet,
I let go, accepting the defeat.
