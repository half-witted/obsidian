## Introduction 
### Definition
- <mark style="background:#d4b106">Vector:</mark> a quantity that has <mark style="background:#d4b106">magnitude</mark> and <mark style="background:#d4b106">direction</mark>
- <mark style="background:#d4b106">Scalar:</mark> a quantity that has <mark style="background:#d4b106">magnitude</mark> with <mark style="background:#d4b106">no direction</mark>
### Determining a Vector
- From 2 points: $A_(x_{A},y_{A})$ and $B_({x_{B},y_{B}})$, $\vec{AB}=\begin{bmatrix}x_{B}-x_{A}\\y_{B}-y_{A}\end{bmatrix}$
- Component form: can be written as $\begin{bmatrix}x\\y\end{bmatrix}$ or $\begin{bmatrix}i\\j\end{bmatrix}$, $(x,y)$ or $(i,j)$
- $\vec{AB}=-\vec{BA}$ 
- $\vec{p}=\frac{n\vec{a}+m\vec{b}}{m+n}$
### Vector Magnitude (Length)
- If $a=\begin{bmatrix}x\\y\end{bmatrix} \to |a|$ $=\sqrt{ x^2+y^2 }$
- $|AB|=|BA|$
## Algebra
### Vector Between Two Points
$A_(x_{A},y_{A})$ and $B_({x_{B},y_{B}}) \to \vec{AB}=\begin{bmatrix}x_{B}-x_{A}\\y_{B}-y_{A}\end{bmatrix}$ or end point $-$ starting point
### Unit Vector
A unit vector has <mark style="background:#d4b106">magnitude 1</mark> that <mark style="background:#d4b106">shows a direction</mark>
$\hat{v} = \frac{1}{|\vec{v}|}\vec{v}$
## Dot Product and Angles
- If $\vec{a} = [a_1, a_2]$  and $\vec{b} = [b_1, b_2,] \to \vec{a} * \vec{b} = a_1b_1 + a_2b_2$
- $\vec{a} * \vec{b} = |a||b|\cos \theta$
## Projection
| <center>Scalar projection</center> | <center>Unit vector</center> | <center>Vector projection</center> |
| :--------------------------------: | :--------------------------: | :--------------------------------: |
|              how much              |       which direction        |     how much + which direction     |
### Scalar Projection
The <mark style="background:#d4b106">length</mark> of $\vec{x}$ in the direction of $\vec{y}$ with <mark style="background:#d4b106">no direction</mark> 
$comp_{\vec{b}}\vec{a} = \frac{\vec{x} * \vec{y}}{|\vec{y}|}$
### Vector Projection
The <mark style="background:#d4b106">actual vector</mark> that represents that part of $\vec{x}$
$proj_{\vec{b}}\vec{a} = \frac{\vec{a} * \vec{b}}{|\vec{b}|^2}\vec{b}$
