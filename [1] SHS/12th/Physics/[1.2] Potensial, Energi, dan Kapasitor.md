## Potensial Listrik
Usaha utk memindahkan 1 muatan antar titik
### Persamaan
| $V=\frac{w}{q}$                                                                           | $V=k\frac{Q}{r}$                                                                                                           | $V=\int E\space dr$                                                                           | $V_{2}-V_{1}=kQ\left( \frac{1}{r_{2}}-\frac{1}{r_{1}} \right)$ |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| $V=$ potensial listrik ($V$)<br>$w=$ usaha berpindah ($J$)<br>$q=$ muatan yg pindah ($C$) | $k=$ konstanta coulomb ($9*10^9\space \frac{Nm^2}{C^2}$)<br>$Q=$ muatan sumber ($C$)<br>$r=$ jarak trhdp suatu titik ($m$) | $E=$ [[[1.1] Muatan dan Medan Listrik\|medan listrik]] ($\frac{N}{C}$)<br>$d=$ *differential* | $r_{1,2}=$ posisi awal, akhir muatan ($m$)                     |
### Resultan

| $V=V_{1}+V_{2}$                                                         | $V=k\frac{Q_{1}}{r_{1}}+k\frac{Q_{2}}{r_{2}}$                                                                                     | $V=\sum k\frac{Q_{i}}{r_{i}}$ |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| $V=$ resultan potensial ($V$)<br>$V_{1,2}=$ potensial muatan 1, 2 ($V$) | $k=$ konstanta coulomb ($9*10^9\space \frac{Nm^2}{C^2}$)<br>$Q_{1,2}=$ muatan 1, 2 ($C$)<br>$r_{1,2}=$ jarak ke muatan 1, 2 ($m$) |                               |
