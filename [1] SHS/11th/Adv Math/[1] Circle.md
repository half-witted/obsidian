## Equation
| <center>General form</center> | <center>Standard form</center> |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| $x^2+y^2+Ax+By+C=0$           | $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$          
### General to Standard
$x^2+y^2-6x+8y-11=0$
1. Group $x$ and $y$
   $(x^2-6x)+(y^2+8y)=11$
2. Perfect square
   $(x-3)^2-9+(y+4)^2-16=11$
3. Move constant
   $(x-3)^2+(y+4)^2=36$
### Standard to General
1. Develop each perfect square
	- $(x-3)^2=x^2-6x+9$
	- $(y+4)^2=y^2+8y+16$
2. Substitute
   $x^2+y^2=-6x+8y+25=36$
3. Move constant
   $x^2+y^2-6x+8y-11=0$
## Centre and Radius
### Centre ($h,k$)
$h=-\frac{A}{2},k=-\frac{B}{2}$
### Radius ($r$)
$r=\sqrt{\left( -\frac{A}{2} \right)^2+\left( -\frac{B}{2} \right)^2-C}$
