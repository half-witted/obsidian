### Derajat dan Radian
Ukuran besar sudut
$\pi=180^{\circ}$

| <center>Derajat</center>                                                                     | <center>Radian</center>                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| $1^{\circ}=\displaystyle\frac{\pi}{180}\space rad$<br>Besar sudut 1 lingkaran $=360^{\circ}$ | $1\pi\space rad=180^{\circ}$<br>Besar sudut 1 lingkaran $=2\pi\space rad$ |
