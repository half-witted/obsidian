## Rumus Trigonometri
1. Identitas:
	- Dasar:
		- $\sin^2x+\cos^2x=1$
		- $1+\tan^2x=\sec^2x$
		- $1+\cot^2x=\csc^2x$
	- Perbandingan:
		- $\cot x=\frac{1}{\tan x}$
		- $\sec x=\frac{1}{\cos x}$
		- $\csc x=\frac{1}{\sin x}$
2. Sudut rangkap: 
	- $\sin 2x=2\sin x\cos x$
	- $\cos ax=1-2\sin^2\frac{a}{2}x$ 
	- $\cos ax=\cos^2\frac{a}{2}x-\sin^2\frac{a}{2}x$
	- $\cos ax=2\cos^2x-1$
3. Jumlah sudut, selisih sudut: $\cos A-\cos B=-2\sin\frac{1}{2}(A+B)\sin\frac{1}{2}(A-B)$
